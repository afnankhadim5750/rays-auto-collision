Just had an accident?

What to do first

The first hour after a bump is stressful. Work through these three things, then let us handle the vehicle.

1 · Make it safe

Check everyone is okay, move the vehicle out of traffic if it is safe to do so, and switch your hazard lights on.

2 · Record the damage

Take clear photos of the damage and the other vehicle, and swap insurance and contact details while everything is fresh.

3 · Get your free estimate

Call (256) 739-1805 or bring the vehicle to the shop in Vinemont, Mon–Fri 8:00 AM – 5:30 PM. We will look it over and give you a free, written estimate — and we can deal with your insurer directly from there.
